The Fool
  Three things came out of your bag and stayed out. That is the decision, made at the door, in about four seconds. A feather on the plate, drawn once. Lightness is not innocence. It is a shorter list.
  Q. What did you leave out?

The Magician
  Every tool you need is on the bench and has been for three weeks. Nothing is missing. A flask on the plate, clean, empty, ready. The missing tool is starting. It is not on the bench because it is not a tool.
  Q. What is already on your bench?

The High Priestess

  Q. What did you already answer?

The Empress

The Emperor
  Everyone mocks the two-signature rule and everyone uses it. You mocked it too. A standing stone on the plate, upright, unornamented. Structure gets its reputation from the people it inconveniences. It gets its value from the week it stops something.
  Q. Which rule did you mock this week?

The Hierophant
  You do Sunday the way it was done in the house you grew up in. The roast, the timing, the argument at four. A skep on the plate, one shape, repeated. You never chose any of it. Most of it is fine.
  Q. Which part did you choose?

The Lovers
  Sending it changes two lives. Not sending it changes one. Your reply has been in drafts for four days. Both of those facts have been true the whole time. Two entwined stems, and no way to unwind them afterwards.
  Q. What is sitting in your drafts?

The Chariot
  Speed you chose feels like control. Speed that chose you feels identical from inside the same car. A spoked wheel on the plate, turning. Both hands are on it. Only one of those two is driving.
  Q. Chosen, or arrived?

Strength
  You are on the floor at the edge of the bed. The cat has not come out. Force has never once worked here. Not once, in the entire history of frightened animals. Strength here is the patience to stay on the floor.
  Q. Who are you waiting for?

The Hermit
  You left the phone in the kitchen for one evening. By nine you had an opinion you did not know you held. A lantern on the plate. Small light, short range. That is the whole method, and it is unglamorous.
  Q. What surfaced by nine?

The Wheel of Fortune

  Q. What did you take credit for?

Justice

  Q. Which column did you skip?

The Hanged Man
  Your train is ninety minutes late and there is nothing to do about it. You did not choose this hour and would never have scheduled it. A chrysalis on the plate, hanging, doing invisible work. The hour counts anyway.
  Q. What did the delay hand you?

Death
  You pulled the dead stems out. The bed is empty now. That emptiness is the work, finished. Something in your life ended this season and you were the one who cleared it. An empty bed in November is not a failed garden.
  Q. What did you clear this season?

Temperance
  Your eleventh version of the soup differs from the tenth by a quarter teaspoon. That is the whole method. It is extremely boring. Two vessels, joined by a stream. Nothing here arrives by a leap.
  Q. What did you adjust by a quarter?

The Devil
  You opened it again without deciding to. That is the whole mechanism and it is not a character flaw. A bramble knot, grown from a hundred separate evenings, none of which was the problem. The knot is still a knot.
  Q. What opens without you deciding?

The Tower
  You built it, you maintained it, and this week you watched it come down. All three of those are true at once, and none of them cancels the others. What you knew and couldn't say is now visible from the road. That part, at least, is over.
  Q. What can you admit now?

The Star
  A tap left barely on, running into a barrel all night. By morning there is four inches in it. A seven-pointed star on the plate. Refill is a schedule and not an event. You have been on one for a fortnight.
  Q. What has been refilling slowly?

The Moon
  At two in the morning it is enormous. At nine it is a phone call and nothing more. Same facts, both times. The difference is not information. Knowing that at nine does not help you at two.
  Q. What is it at nine?

The Sun

  Q. Which day was it?

Judgement

  Q. Which worry no longer reaches you?

The World

  Q. What did you just finish?

Ace of Hawthorn

  Q. What arrived early this week?

Two of Hawthorn

  Q. Which horizon keeps coming back?

Three of Hawthorn

Four of Hawthorn

Five of Hawthorn

Six of Hawthorn

  Q. Who paid you a compliment you deflected?

Seven of Hawthorn

  Q. Who is still arguing with you, actually?

Eight of Hawthorn
  Recorded, eight things in the air. Sent, unanswered, in transit. All out of your hands. The week is moving faster than its replies, which is not the same as moving faster than its work. Speed of dispatch is not speed of arrival.
  Q. What is out of your hands right now?

Nine of Hawthorn

  Q. How far is actually left?

Ten of Hawthorn

  Q. Which one did nobody actually hand you?

Page of Hawthorn

Knight of Hawthorn

Queen of Hawthorn

King of Hawthorn

Ace of Bellflower

Two of Bellflower

Three of Bellflower

Four of Bellflower

  Q. What have you stopped seeing?

Five of Bellflower

Six of Bellflower

Seven of Bellflower

Eight of Bellflower

Nine of Bellflower

Queen of Bellflower
